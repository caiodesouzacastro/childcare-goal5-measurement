\\[3pt]{\footnotesize \textit{Note:} Ratio = (share applying before 12 months) $/$ (share ever in the queue), by cohort. Because both use the same live-births denominator, the ratio equals the share of those who ever register whose first application occurs before 12 months. The denominator is \emph{not} bounded by a common age: it accumulates registrations over whatever part of the eligibility window each cohort is observed for, and is right-censored in the most recent cohorts, so part of the movement in the ratio reflects unequal follow-up rather than timing. The common-age version of this statistic, with both sides capped at 24 months, is in Table~\ref{tab:P5timing}.}
