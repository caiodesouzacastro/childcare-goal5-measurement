The recurring releases report stocks and, for childcare, a binary split of the wait
(up to versus over 30 days); they do not report the distribution of age at
application, an exact or mean waiting duration, or the entry, referral and exit
flows that generate the stock. Periodicity differs between the historical series
and the current releases: the quarterly snapshots plotted in
Figure~\ref{fig:F10} are the historical series, whereas the recurring release
current at the time of writing is bimonthly. Sources: the secretariat's Demanda
Escolar reporting \citep{sme_demanda_escolar}, the S\~ao Paulo Open Data Portal
series on registered demand and enrolments (EOL) \citep{sme_dados_abertos}, a
representative bimonthly district release \citep{sme_demanda_junho2026}, and the
``Vaga na Creche'' platform \citep{sme_vaga_creche}; all accessed 24 July 2026.}
