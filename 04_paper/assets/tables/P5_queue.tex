\\[3pt]{\footnotesize \textit{Note:} A single reconstruction on the first-application universe. Q$_0$: stock at 1 January (children who had applied and not yet exited), from the micro data itself, not from the official series. new: first applications in the year. ref/aged/des: first exits in the year by referral, by aging out at 47 months, and by recorded withdrawal. Q$_t$: year-end stock, counted directly. The accounting identity Q$_t$ $=$ Q$_{t-1}$ $+$ new $-$ ref $-$ aged $-$ des closes from the flows themselves; the internal residual (col.\ int.) is at most a few hundred children (edge-of-year timing and refusals-without-flag), \emph{not} a plug defined to close. off: official December registered demand (DEM\_CRECHE). gap: Q$_t$ minus official. The micro flows do not reproduce the published stock. The 2010 gap is largely mechanical: the pre-panel backlog is missing from Q$_0$, so the reconstruction starts far below the true stock. The persistent gaps thereafter, between $-6$ and $-17$ percent, narrowing over the period but never closing, are the informative ones: they indicate that the published stock is a composite administrative object---incorporating re-registrations, unrecorded removals and a broader universe---rather than a reproducible count of first-application waiting.}
