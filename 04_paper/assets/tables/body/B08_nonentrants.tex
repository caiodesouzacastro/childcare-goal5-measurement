\tabnote{\emph{Source:} SME childcare request record. \emph{Sample:} birth cohorts 2010 and 2014. Row 1: children who enrolled at or before 48 months (as in Table~\ref{tab:adjpairs}). Rows 2--3 add applicants of the cohort who reached 47 months without a referral (27.4 percent of 2010-cohort applicants and 5.2 percent in 2014; Table~\ref{tab:D4}). \emph{Variables:} changes in mean age at application and at entry, months. Entry age is undefined for a child who never enters, so it is imputed at two extremes: the 48-month ceiling on age at entry, or the child's age at application (no wait). \emph{Exercise:} accounting identity in means; the two rows are extreme imputations, not bounds on an estimand. Both imputations give an application share above 100 percent, that is, a waiting component of the opposite sign: the sign depends on whether non-entrants are counted, not on how they are imputed. Descriptive.}
